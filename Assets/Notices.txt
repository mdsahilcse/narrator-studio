Narrator Studio — local audio components

Speech synthesis: Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base, by the Qwen team (Apache 2.0).
8-bit MLX conversion by mlx-community. The model files are distributed unchanged.
https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base
https://huggingface.co/mlx-community/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base-8bit

Transcription: OpenAI Whisper (MIT), converted to CTranslate2 by Systran.
https://github.com/openai/whisper
https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-base

Speaker measurement: WeSpeaker VoxCeleb ResNet34-LM, by the WeSpeaker authors,
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Uses the ONNX conversion distributed
in csukuangfj/speaker-embedding-models. The ONNX file is unchanged.
https://huggingface.co/Wespeaker/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/speaker-embedding-models
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The Python runtime includes license files and metadata shipped by its packages.
The packaged FFmpeg executable is distributed by imageio-ffmpeg.
https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg
https://ffmpeg.org/
